My Little Demon

A letter I needed you to read.

For Rriann

My Little Demon,

I don't really know how to begin this, because a part of me still doesn't want to believe that this is where our journey ends.

But maybe some journeys aren't meant to last forever. Maybe some people come into our lives not to stay until the end, but to change us, teach us, and leave something beautiful behind.

And that's what you did for me.

I'm truly thankful that I met you, Rriann. You changed me in ways I never thought someone could. You helped me see my mistakes, you helped me become better, and you showed me parts of myself that I needed to fix. For all of that, I will always be grateful to you.

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for all the mistakes I've made, for all the words I said that I shouldn't have, and for every moment I made you feel like you weren't enough or that you were worth nothing.

You never deserved to feel that way.

I know I promised you before that I would change and become better. I meant it when I said it. But I also understand that sometimes, promises come too late when the same mistakes have already caused too much pain.

And maybe this is where I finally have to accept that.

I love you. I really do.

But I've learned that sometimes love alone isn't enough when the person you love keeps getting hurt by your actions. I don't want to keep hurting you while telling you that I love you.

So this time, I'm not asking for another chance.

I'm not asking you to forgive me.

Even if you can't accept my apology, I just hope that someday you'll know that I truly regretted the things I did and the things I said. I want you to know that I'm genuinely sorry not because I'm losing you, but because I hurt someone who meant so much to me.

I'll always remember the little things we shared.

I'll remember our Minecraft world—the things we built together, the way we'd help each other, the way we'd look at the sunset, laugh together, and spend those late nights talking about anything and everything.

Those moments might look simple to someone else, but to me, they were pieces of a life I was happy to have.

I'll cherish them.

Thank you for loving me through my highs and lows.

Thank you for staying with me, even if our time together was shorter than we hoped.

Thank you for giving me your time even when you were busy.

Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every late-night talk, every little moment, and every memory.

Thank you for choosing to spend a part of your life with me.

Now, I want you to look forward.

I hope you have a beautiful future ahead of you.

I hope you accomplish every dream you've ever told me about. I hope you become the person you've always wanted to be. I hope you find happiness in places I could never give you.

And most importantly, I hope you're always safe.

Please take care of yourself.

Even if one day I'm no longer beside you, I'll still be wishing that life treats you gently.

I know I'll miss you.

I'll probably miss the little things the most—the laughs, the conversations, building things together, watching sunsets in our little Minecraft world, and simply having you there.

But I don't want my goodbye to be something painful that you have to carry.

I want it to be a thank you.

Thank you for being a part of my story, Rriann.

You were once my Little Demon, but you became someone who taught me how to become a better person.

And wherever life takes us from here, I'll always be grateful that our paths crossed.

This is the end of our journey.

Not because what we had meant nothing,
but because it meant enough for me to finally let you go.

I won't ask you to come back.

I won't ask you for another chance.

I'll just quietly wish you happiness from wherever life takes me.

I love you, Rriann.

And even though I have to let you go, a part of me will always be thankful that I got to love you.

And thank you for reading this.
